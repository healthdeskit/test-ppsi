Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information PPSI may collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our website, forms, and services.

Last updated: March 2026 Applies to PPSI websites and online forms

Information we collect

  • Contact information: name, phone, email, and basic request details when you submit forms.
  • Appointment details: preferred location/date and message content you provide.
  • Technical data: limited website analytics, browser/device information, and referral source data.

How we use your information

  • Schedule appointments, respond to requests, and provide service support.
  • Coordinate communications about locations, follow-up, and care access.
  • Maintain security, improve website performance, and prevent misuse.

Data sharing and protection

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Information is shared only when needed for operations, legal compliance, or patient service workflows.
  • We use administrative and technical safeguards to reduce unauthorized access risks.

Your choices

  • You can contact us to update or correct submitted contact details.
  • You may request clarification on how your form data is being processed.
  • For urgent clinical matters, please call directly instead of using web forms.

Need help?

For privacy-related questions, records requests, or policy clarification, contact our team and include enough detail so we can route your request correctly.
