Dr. Patrick Nierva

Chiropractor

Book Appointment Our Locations

Dr. Patrick Nierva is a licensed chiropractor at Precision Pain & Spine Institute (PPSI). He treats back pain, neck pain, headaches, and joint stiffness with spinal adjustments, mobilization, and soft-tissue therapy. His approach is practical and patient-focused: he explains your condition clearly and works with PPSI's pain management and physical therapy teams when you need coordinated care.

Clinical focus

Spine & musculoskeletal care

Spinal manipulation, joint mobilization, and soft-tissue work for low back and neck pain, sciatica, tension headaches, posture-related strain, and stiffness. Dr. Nierva provides ergonomic and home-care guidance when helpful.

About Dr. Patrick Nierva

Dr. Patrick Nierva is a New Jersey-licensed chiropractor at Precision Pain & Spine Institute. He focuses on comfortable, goal-oriented care: understanding your pain, treating it with hands-on techniques, and coordinating with other PPSI specialists when your case needs a broader approach.

Expertise

Dr. Nierva treats low back and neck pain, disc problems, headaches, joint stiffness, sciatica, and posture-related issues. He tailors each treatment plan to your symptoms and daily activities.
