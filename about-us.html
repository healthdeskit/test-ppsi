About PPSI

A physician-led New Jersey practice built around access, trust, and coordinated care.

Precision Pain & Spine Institute brings together specialists, modern facilities, and a long-term commitment to the communities we serve, so patients experience a more connected path from diagnosis through treatment and recovery.

Physician-led organization Multi-location New Jersey footprint Coordinated specialty care
Our Story

Physician-led care with closer coordination.

Precision Pain & Spine Institute was established by clinicians who saw a gap in how pain and spine care were delivered across New Jersey. Rather than sending patients between disconnected offices, we built a practice where physicians and providers coordinate care more closely.

The organization began with a smaller footprint and expanded carefully into additional communities over time. Each new office has been developed to reflect the same clinical standards, operational consistency, and patient experience expectations.

We remain physician-led. That structure helps keep decision-making grounded in patient care, long-term quality, and the practical realities of running a strong regional practice.

What We Stand For

Values that guide how we operate and grow.

Integrity

We aim to make clinical recommendations based on patient need, treatment appropriateness, and long-term outcomes.

Access

With multiple offices across New Jersey and transportation support in certain situations, we work to reduce barriers to care.

Stability

Consistent leadership, structured operations, and staff development help create a stronger experience for both patients and teams.

Community

We see our offices as part of the neighborhoods they serve and continue building around the needs of New Jersey communities.

Our Facilities

Modern spaces designed for comfort, efficiency, and consistency.

Each PPSI location is designed to support a cleaner, calmer, and more organized patient experience. We invest in exam rooms, updated equipment, and workflows that support both comfort and clinical efficiency.

Our Edison campus operates as a major hub with broader administrative and clinical support, while other sites are built to maintain the same standards of quality, process, and patient communication.

Our Culture

Teams and handoffs that stay aligned.

PPSI operates through collaboration. Pain specialists, surgeons, chiropractors, and support teams work through coordinated processes so handoffs feel smoother and treatment planning stays more consistent.

We also value continuing education, professional development, and internal alignment. Stronger systems and better-trained teams support better patient experiences.

Workplace culture matters as well. A stable, respectful environment helps staff perform better and helps patients feel more comfortable throughout their care journey.

Growth & Commitment

New locations only when quality stays the same.

Our growth has been shaped by community demand, operational readiness, and the ability to maintain consistent standards across locations. Expansion is approached deliberately rather than for scale alone.

As the practice evolves, the goal remains the same: extend access where there is real need while preserving the quality, coordination, and physician-led structure that define the organization.

Precision Pain & Spine Institute continues to build for the long term as a practice focused on New Jersey patients, New Jersey communities, and sustainable clinical growth.
