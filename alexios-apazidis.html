Dr. Alexios Apazidis M.D.

Orthopedic Spine Surgeon

Dr. Alexios Apazidis is an Orthopedic Spine Surgeon at Precision Pain & Spine Institute with specialized expertise in spine surgery and advanced structural treatment planning. He performs minimally invasive procedures, disc replacement, fusion, and revision surgeries for patients with complex spine conditions across New Jersey. His surgical approach prioritizes precision, reduced recovery time, and lasting outcomes.

About Dr. Alexios Apazidis & Spine Surgery Expertise

Dr. Alexios Apazidis brings advanced surgical training and years of experience to Precision Pain & Spine Institute. As an orthopedic spine surgeon, he focuses on conditions that require surgical intervention when conservative care has not provided sufficient relief. He works closely with PPSI's pain management team, chiropractors, and physical therapists to ensure that surgery is considered at the right time and that postoperative rehabilitation is seamless.

His practice emphasizes minimally invasive techniques whenever possible. Smaller incisions, reduced muscle disruption, and advanced instrumentation help many patients return to daily activities faster and with less postoperative pain. For complex cases such as revision spine surgery, spinal deformity, or multi-level pathology, Dr. Apazidis draws on his extensive training to develop individualized surgical plans that align with each patient's anatomy and goals.

Expertise & Specializations

Dr. Apazidis specializes in lumbar and cervical spine surgery, including discectomy, laminectomy, spinal fusion, artificial disc replacement, and treatment of spinal stenosis, disc herniation, spondylolisthesis, and degenerative disc disease. He is experienced in both traditional open and minimally invasive approaches and selects the most appropriate technique based on the patient's condition and imaging findings.

He also treats scoliosis, kyphosis, and other spinal deformities, as well as revision surgeries for patients who have had prior spine surgery. His commitment to continuous learning keeps him current with emerging technologies and evidence-based surgical innovations that improve safety and outcomes.

Education, Credentials & Training

Dr. Alexios Apazidis completed medical school, orthopedic residency, and fellowship training in spine surgery. He holds board certification in orthopaedic surgery and maintains active hospital privileges and professional memberships. His training includes advanced techniques in minimally invasive spine surgery, motion preservation, and complex spinal reconstruction.

At PPSI, he ensures that surgical decisions are made collaboratively with patients and that expectations regarding recovery, rehabilitation, and long-term results are clearly communicated. Preoperative education and coordinated postoperative care are integral to his practice.

Approach to Patient Care

Dr. Apazidis believes that spine surgery should be reserved for carefully selected patients after a thorough evaluation and trial of non-surgical options when appropriate. He takes time to explain imaging findings, discuss surgical and non-surgical alternatives, and answer questions so that patients can make informed decisions. His goal is to restore function, reduce pain, and support a return to an active lifestyle.

He coordinates closely with referring physicians and the PPSI multidisciplinary team to ensure continuity of care before and after surgery. Rehabilitation protocols are tailored to each procedure and patient, with a focus on safe progression and sustainable results.

Conditions Treated by Dr. Apazidis

Dr. Apazidis treats a range of spine conditions requiring surgical evaluation or intervention, including:

  • Lumbar and cervical disc herniation
  • Spinal stenosis
  • Degenerative disc disease
  • Spondylolisthesis
  • Spinal instability
  • Failed back surgery syndrome (revision)
  • Spinal deformities (scoliosis, kyphosis)
  • Fractures and trauma
  • Spinal infections
  • Artificial disc replacement candidacy

Why Patients Choose Dr. Alexios Apazidis for Spine Surgery

Patients and referring physicians choose Dr. Apazidis for his expertise in minimally invasive spine surgery, clear communication, and commitment to optimal outcomes. He takes a conservative approach to surgery, recommending it only when non-surgical options have been appropriately exhausted or when the condition warrants immediate intervention. His collaborative style and attention to postoperative rehabilitation help patients feel confident throughout their care journey.

Where to See Dr. Alexios Apazidis in New Jersey

Dr. Apazidis sees patients at Precision Pain & Spine Institute locations across New Jersey. Surgical procedures may be performed at affiliated hospitals or ambulatory surgery centers.
