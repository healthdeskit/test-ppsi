Current Position

Precision Pain & Spine Institute
New Jersey Interventional Pain Management Specialist — Present

Dr. Elkholy has dealt with a wide range of conditions: Low Back Pain, Neck Pain, Mid Back Pain, Work Injuries, Automobile Accidents, Whiplash, Sciatica, Sports Injuries, Arthritis, Overuse Injuries (Repetitive Stress Injuries). Extensive expertise in minimally invasive open lumbar discectomy. Proctor to educate pain management and orthopedic spine surgeons in minimally invasive open lumbar discectomies.

  • Uses evidence-based pain management for car accident patients
  • Counseled patients on lifestyle changes (medication, exercise, sleep habits) to enhance treatment success
  • Maintained detailed health records and evaluated treatment outcomes
  • Performed 600+ open lumbar discectomies, 400+ cervical discectomies, 300+ cervical & lumbar endoscopic rhizotomies
  • 500 minimally invasive discectomy surgeries in the last four years
  • One of the few interventional pain management physicians in the tri-state area performing cervical discectomy at high volume

Procedures Performed

Discectomy (Open & Percutaneous); Minimally Invasive Discectomy; Cervical Discectomy; Cervical Craniotomy; Lumbar Anatomy; Lumbar Transforaminal Fusion & Facet Screw Implant; Spinal Cord Stimulator Implant; Physical Therapy; Laser Surgery; Epidural Steroid Injections (Transforaminal & Interlaminar); Radiofrequency Ablations; Discography; Joint Injections; Medial Branch Blocks; Selective Nerve Blocks; Trigger Point Injections; Subcostal, Orbital, Trigeminal Nerve Blocks; Facet Joint Injections; Acupuncture.
