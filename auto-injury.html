Why See a Specialist After an Auto Accident?

Neck and back pain from a car accident, including whiplash and soft-tissue injury, often need structured evaluation and treatment. Waiting or ignoring symptoms can lead to chronic pain. Early care can reduce inflammation, restore movement, and document your injuries for insurance or legal purposes. We provide that care in one place: evaluation, imaging, pain procedures, chiropractic, and PT, with clear documentation every step of the way.

Conditions We Treat

We commonly treat whiplash, neck and back pain, headaches, shoulder and arm pain, and other musculoskeletal symptoms that start or worsen after a motor vehicle accident. We also evaluate for more serious injury (e.g., fracture or nerve damage) and refer when needed. Our goal is to get you better and support your recovery with evidence-based treatment and clear records.

Insurance and Documentation

We work with most auto insurance and personal injury protection (PIP) plans. We keep detailed records of your visits, diagnosis, and treatment so you have what you need for your claim or for your attorney. Our staff can help with referrals, authorizations, and forms so you're not navigating paperwork alone while you're in pain.

How Long Will I Need Care?

It depends on your injuries and how you respond. Some people improve in a few weeks with a short course of care; others need longer treatment. We re-evaluate regularly and adjust your plan. We don't prolong care unnecessarily, we aim to get you better and document your recovery fairly and clearly.

Coordinated Care Under One Roof

Pain management, chiropractic, and physical therapy are all part of PPSI. That means one team, one plan, and one set of records. You don't have to coordinate multiple offices or worry that your care is fragmented. We communicate internally so your treatment is consistent and your documentation is complete.
