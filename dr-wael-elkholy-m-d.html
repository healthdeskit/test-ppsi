Patient-Centered Care

Comprehensive evaluation and evidence-based treatment.

Dr. Elkholy begins with a comprehensive exam to locate your pain, reviews symptoms, and medical history. He performs a detailed physical exam, paying close attention to the function and movement of the injured or damaged area. He uses evidence-based pain management approaches and counsels patients on lifestyle changes (medication, exercise, pain management techniques, sleep habits) to enhance treatment success and improve overall health.

Hands-On Care

One-on-one attention for accurate diagnosis and treatment.

Dr. Elkholy takes time with each patient to understand symptoms, review imaging, and develop a personalized care plan. His hands-on approach and attention to detail help patients move toward lasting relief.

Clinical focus

Areas of expertise

Board-certified interventional care, from accurate imaging review through targeted procedures and recovery support.

Acute & chronic pain management

Plans tailored for new injuries and long-standing pain, guided by your imaging and nerve studies.

  • Review of MRI & EMG to pinpoint the source of symptoms
  • Cervical & lumbar epidural steroid injections
  • Radiofrequency ablation when longer-lasting relief is appropriate

Discectomy & rhizotomy

Surgical and endoscopic options when conservative care isn’t enough.

  • Open lumbar & cervical discectomy for disc-related nerve compression
  • Endoscopic rhizotomy (cervical & lumbar) to address facet-mediated pain

Interventional procedures

Precision injections and diagnostic blocks to clarify and treat pain generators.

  • Discography & selective nerve blocks
  • Medial branch & facet joint injections
  • Joint injections, trigger point treatment & related blocks

Acupuncture & rehabilitation

Whole-person support alongside procedures, movement, recovery, and targeted nerve care.

  • Coordination with physical therapy & activity planning
  • Specialized blocks: subcostal, orbital, trigeminal
  • Laser-assisted options when clinically appropriate
